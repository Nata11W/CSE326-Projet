      Useful if you don't use the internet often, need to reschedule, or have a question before booking.

        How booking works

        1. Create a free account with your name, email and phone number.
        2. Choose a doctor by name or specialty, and look at their profile.
        3. Pick a free time and confirm. You get a token slip with your queue number.
        4. Come to the clinic on time. You can cancel online or call the clinic to change it.
