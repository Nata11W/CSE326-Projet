      How booking works

      1. Create a free account with your name, email and phone number.
      2. Choose a doctor by specialty, and look at their profile and photo.
      3. Pick a free time and pay the ₹20 fee online or at the clinic.
      4. Show your token slip at the clinic. Cancel online or call to change it.
