Book your doctor in under a minute, any time of day.

WebCareBooking replaces phone queues with a simple online calendar. Patients see real free slots; clinics stop managing appointments by hand.

Why WebCareBooking?

Open 24/7

Book or cancel at midnight or on a Sunday. No phone calls, no waiting on hold.

Real-time slots

Only truly free times are shown, and a slot disappears the moment someone books it.

Fewer no-shows

Browser reminders alert patients before their visit, so fewer appointments are missed.

Less admin work

Clinics set working hours once; the calendar builds itself and the dashboard tracks everything.

How it works

  1. Create an account with your name, email and phone.
  2. Choose a doctor and a date.
  3. Pick a free time slot and confirm.
  4. Get a reminder and manage your bookings anytime.
